# FXUS Ecosystem

### Building an Independent Global Digital Technology Ecosystem

**Digital Wallets | Blockchain Technology | Digital Assets | Trading Infrastructure | Node Systems**

Welcome to the official GitHub organization of **FXUS Ecosystem**.

FXUS Ecosystem is an independent technology initiative focused on building modular infrastructure for digital wallets, blockchain technology, digital assets, trading platforms, and node systems.

## Our Vision

To develop a secure, scalable, interoperable, and globally accessible digital technology ecosystem.

## Core Ecosystem Projects

### 1. V.WALLET
A digital wallet platform under development, designed as a central access point for FXUS Ecosystem services.

### 2. FXUS Token / Coin
A planned digital asset initiative covering token infrastructure, free core mining, pre-sale mechanisms, market access, and future blockchain development.

### 3. FXUS Trading
An independently developed trading technology platform. Any future brokerage operations will be subject to applicable licensing and regulatory approvals.

### 4. FXUS Node
A planned node infrastructure initiative supporting future ecosystem services and distributed technology development.
